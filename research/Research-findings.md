# Research Findings
Date: 09/05/26

## Survey summary
- Respondents: 22
- Top barrier: Overwhelm by choices — 68%
- Second barrier: Don't understand how it works — 62%
- Money cited as barrier: only 30%
- Solution validation: 73% said personalised 
  recommendation would make them invest

## Review mining summary
- Total reviews analyzed: 40
- Category 1 — Overwhelm / Choice paralysis: 38%
- Category 2 — Knowledge gap: 27%
- Category 3 — Fear / Trust: 18%
- Category 4 — UX / Technical issues: 12%
- Category 5 — Positive feedback: 5%

## Hypothesis validation
- H1 (Overwhelm is primary barrier): Validated — 38%
- H2 (Money is NOT the barrier): Validated — only 30%
- H3 (Personalisation solves it): Partially revised — 
  trust layer also needed

## 5 Key insights
1. 68% of non-investors cite overwhelm, not money, 
   as top barrier. Solution must reduce decisions, 
   not add information.

2. 59% downloaded an app but never transacted. 
   Acquisition works — activation is broken.

3. 73% said a personalised recommendation would 
   make them invest immediately. Validates 
   goal-based onboarding direction.

4. ~18% of reviews cite fear of loss as a barrier. 
   Solution needs trust signals, not just 
   simplified choice.

5. 50% would start SIP at ₹100–500/month. 
   Default SIP amount should be ₹100, not ₹1000.

## Revised problem statement
The primary barrier to first investment is cognitive 
overload from an undifferentiated fund catalogue 
(38% of surveyed users) combined with fear of 
financial loss (18% of reviews). The barrier is 
NOT financial — only 13% cited affordability. 
Solution must reduce decisions AND build trust.
